## General Rules

- Keep all code changes coherent with the rest of the repository. Avoid temporary or isolated patches. Every change should preserve the structural, stylistic, and grammatical consistency of the surrounding codebase.
- When writing or editing text, always follow the writing guide below:
  - `.local/writing-guideline.md`
  - Avoid middle dots and em dashes unless they are necessary.
  - Maintain a consistent language and tone across the page.
  - Distinguish clearly between UI copy and body copy. UI copy should use a concise, fragment-based style, while body copy  use complete, styled sentences where appropriate.
